#### Section 2: The Paael Stem

**Core Meaning: Intensive and Factitive**

The Paael stem (also spelled Pael or Pāʿel) expresses two related types
of intensified meaning:

- **Intensive:** Repeated, thoroughgoing, or heightened action. \'He
  wrote\' (Peal) → \'he wrote carefully/copied out\' (Paael).

- **Factitive:** Causing someone or something to enter into a state.
  \'Be holy\' (Peal) → \'make holy/sanctify\' (Paael). The Paael turns
  intransitive verbs into transitive ones.

**Formation: Gemination of the Middle Root Letter**

The defining feature of the Paael is the **doubling (gemination) of the
middle root consonant (R2)**. This is written with a *dageš*-like
diacritic in pointing, or simply by writing the letter twice in some
scripts.

- **Perfect:** CaR₁R₂R₂eR₃ pattern --- e.g., barrek (from b-r-k), qaddaš
  (from q-d-š), yallep (from y-l-p)

- **Participle:** mCaR₁R₂R₂eR₃ pattern (m- prefix) --- e.g., mbarrek,
  mqaddeš, myallep

**Cross-Linguistic Comparison**

- **Hebrew Piel:** The direct cognate of the Syriac Paael. Also formed
  by doubling R2. Compare: *kipper* (Piel, \'atone/cover\' --- from
  k-p-r), *bērak* (Piel, \'bless\' --- from b-r-k), *qiddēš* (Piel,
  \'sanctify\'). The structural identity is near-perfect.

- **Arabic Form II:** Formed by doubling R2 (qaddama, \'to
  present/offer\'; ʿallama, \'to teach/instruct\'; qaddasa, \'to
  sanctify\'). Arabic Form II corresponds precisely to Syriac Paael in
  both form and function.

- **Greek:** No direct morphological equivalent. Intensives expressed
  via prefix verbs (e.g., *κατα-* compounds), aspect (perfect), or
  adverbs. Latin uses intensifying prefixes or separate lexical items.

**Formation Notes**

- **Root:** b-r-k. In the Paael, R2 (resh, r) is geminated: b-rr-k →
  barrek

- **Perfect vowel pattern:** a under R1, doubled R2, e vowel between R2
  and R3 (same as Peal CeCC but with geminate R2)

- **Suffix set:** Essentially identical to Peal and Aphel perfect
  suffixes

- **Guttural roots:** When R2 is a guttural (Ayin, Ḥet, He, Alaph),
  gemination may be \'virtual\' --- compensatory lengthening of the
  preceding vowel occurs instead

**Comparison: Hebrew Piel Perfect**

#### Section 4: Paael Participle Paradigm

**Root: b-r-k \| Stem: Paael \| Meaning: \'one who blesses\'**

The Paael active participle takes the same m- prefix as all derived-stem
participles. The gemination of R2 is preserved throughout.

  ------------ --------------------- --------------------- -------------------------
    **Form**      **Estrangela**      **Transliteration**       **Translation**

   **m.sg.**     [ܡܒܪܟ]{dir="rtl"}         *mbarrek*           blessing (m.sg.)

   **f.sg.**    [ܡܒܪܟܐ]{dir="rtl"}         *mbarrkā*           blessing (f.sg.)

   **m.pl.**    [ܡܒܪܟܝܢ]{dir="rtl"}       *mbarrkīn*           blessing (m.pl.)

   **f.pl.**    [ܡܒܪܟܢ]{dir="rtl"}        *mbarrkān*           blessing (f.pl.)

  ------------ --------------------- --------------------- -------------------------

**Formation Notes**

- **Prefix:** m- (identical to Aphel participle prefix)

- **Vowel pattern:** m + a + R1R2R2 + e + R3 (preserves gemination of
  R2)

- **Suffixes:** Standard participle endings: m.sg. (unmarked), f.sg.
  (-ā), m.pl. (-īn), f.pl. (-ān)

**Comparison with Hebrew and Arabic**

**Hebrew Piel participle:** prefix מְ (mĕ-) + doubled R2. E.g., *mĕbārēk*
(blessing, from b-r-k Piel), *mĕqaddēš* (sanctifying, from q-d-š Piel).
Structurally identical to Syriac Paael participle.

**Arabic Form II active participle:** pattern muCaCCiC (mu + R1 + a +
R2R2 + i + R3). E.g., *muʿallim* (teacher, from ʿ-l-m Form II),
*muqaddis* (sanctifier, from q-d-s Form II). The m- prefix and
gemination are pan-Semitic features.

#### Section 6: The Story --- Part 6

**Yukhannan Blesses the People and Angers the Priests**

**Narrative note:** This passage is rich in Paael forms (barrek, ṣallī,
šabbeḥ, yallep, qaddaš, šammeš). It also introduces the central conflict
of the Acts: Yukhannan\'s ministry challenges the established religious
order. Notice the theological contrast between sacrifice (qūrbānā) and
faith (haymānūttā).

> *Key Paael forms: barrek (blessed), ṣallī (prayed), šabbeḥ (praised),
> yallep (taught), qaddaš (sanctified), šammeš (minister), šabbeḥ
> (praised again at end). Note also metqaddeš --- the Ethpaael (passive
> of Paael), previewing Lesson 7.*

**Sentence 1**

[ܘܝܘܚܢܢ ܩܡ ܥܠ ܡܕܒܚܐ ܘܒܪܟ ܠܥܡܐ]{dir="rtl"}

*w-Yukhannan qam ʿal madbḥā w-barrek l-ʿammā*

w- (and-) Yukhannan (John) qam (stood) ʿal (upon) madbḥā (the-altar)
w-barrek (and-blessed) l-ʿammā (the-people)

*→ And Yukhannan stood at the altar and blessed the people.*

**Sentence 2**

[ܘܨܠܝ ܒܐܕܢܐ ܪܡܐ ܘܫܒܚ ܠܐܠܗܐ]{dir="rtl"}

*w-ṣallī b-ʾednā rāmā w-šabbeḥ l-Alāhā*

w-ṣallī (and-prayed) b-ʾednā (with-voice) rāmā (loud) w-šabbeḥ
(and-praised) l-Alāhā (God)

*→ He prayed with a loud voice and praised God.*

**Sentence 3**

[ܘܝܠܦ ܠܗܘܢ ܐܘܪܚܐ ܚܕܬܐ ܕܨܠܘܬܐ]{dir="rtl"}

*w-yallep lhūn ʾūrḥā ḥattā d-ṣlūttā*

w-yallep (and-taught) lhūn (them) ʾūrḥā (the-way) ḥattā (new) d-ṣlūttā
(of-prayer)

*→ He taught them the new way of prayer.*

**Sentence 4**

[ܘܐܡܪ ܠܐ ܡܕܒܚܐ ܕܟܗܢܘܬܐ ܗܘ ܕܩܕܫ ܐܢܫ]{dir="rtl"}

*w-ʾemar: lā madbḥā d-kahnūttā hū d-qaddaš ʾenāš*

w-ʾemar (and-said) lā (not) madbḥā (the-altar) d-kahnūttā
(of-the-priesthood) hū (it-is) d-qaddaš (that-sanctifies) ʾenāš
(a-person)

*→ \'It is not the altar of the priesthood that sanctifies a person.\'*

**Sentence 5**

[ܐܠܐ ܡܪܝܐ ܗܘ ܕܩܕܫ ܠܟܘܢ ܒܪܘܚܗ]{dir="rtl"}

*ʾellā Māryā hū d-qaddaš lḵūn b-rūḥeh*

ʾellā (but/rather) Māryā (the-Lord) hū (he-is) d-qaddaš (who-sanctifies)
lḵūn (you(pl.)) b-rūḥeh (by-his-Spirit)

*→ \'Rather, it is the Lord who sanctifies you by his Spirit.\'*

**Sentence 6**

[ܫܡܥܘ ܕܝܢ ܟܗܢܐ ܡܠܘܗܝ ܘܐܬܪܓܙܘ ܥܠܘܗܝ]{dir="rtl"}

*šmeʿū dēn kāhnē mellāw-hy w-ʾetragzū ʿlāw-hy*

šmeʿū (heard) dēn (but/now) kāhnē (the-priests) mellāw-hy (his-words)
w-ʾetragzū (and-were-angered) ʿlāw-hy (against-him)

*→ But the priests heard his words and were angered against him.*

**Sentence 7**

[ܘܐܡܪܘ ܡܢ ܝܗܒ ܠܟ ܫܘܠܛܢܐ ܕܫܡܫ ܒܗܝܟܠܐ]{dir="rtl"}

*w-ʾemrū: man yahab lāḵ šulṭānā d-šammeš b-hayklā?*

w-ʾemrū (and-they-said) man (who) yahab (gave) lāḵ (to-you) šulṭānā
(authority) d-šammeš (to-minister) b-hayklā (in-the-temple)

*→ \'Who gave you authority to minister in the temple?\'*

**Sentence 8**

[ܥܢܐ ܝܘܚܢܢ ܘܐܡܪ ܐܠܗܐ ܫܕܪܢܝ ܕܐܩܕܫ ܠܥܡܗ]{dir="rtl"}

*ʿnā Yukhannan w-ʾemar: Alāhā šedarnī d-ʾqaddeš l-ʿammeh*

ʿnā (answered) Yukhannan (Yukhannan) w-ʾemar (and-said) Alāhā (God)
šedarnī (sent-me) d-ʾqaddeš (to-sanctify) l-ʿammeh (his-people)

*→ Yukhannan answered: \'God sent me to sanctify his people.\'*

**Sentence 9**

[ܠܐ ܒܩܘܪܒܢܐ ܐܠܐ ܒܗܝܡܢܘܬܐ ܡܬܩܕܫ ܐܢܫ]{dir="rtl"}

*lā b-qūrbānā ʾellā b-haymānūttā metqaddeš ʾenāš*

lā (not) b-qūrbānā (by-sacrifice) ʾellā (but) b-haymānūttā (by-faith)
metqaddeš (is-sanctified) ʾenāš (a-person)

*→ \'A person is not sanctified by sacrifice but by faith.\'*

**Sentence 10**

[ܘܪܓܙ ܪܒ ܟܗܢܐ ܘܦܩܕ ܕܢܦܩܘܢ ܠܗ ܡܢ ܗܝܟܠܐ]{dir="rtl"}

*w-regaz rab kāhnē w-pqad d-nuppqūn leh men hayklā*

w-regaz (and-was-enraged) rab (chief) kāhnē (of-priests) w-pqad
(and-commanded) d-nuppqūn (that-they-expel) leh (him) men (from) hayklā
(the-temple)

*→ And the chief priest was enraged and commanded that they expel him
from the temple.*

**Sentence 11**

[ܝܘܚܢܢ ܕܝܢ ܠܐ ܕܚܠ ܐܠܐ ܫܒܚ ܠܐܠܗܐ ܒܟܠܗ ܠܒܗ]{dir="rtl"}

*Yukhannan dēn lā dḥel ʾellā šabbeḥ l-Alāhā b-kulleh lebbeh*

Yukhannan (Yukhannan) dēn (but/however) lā (not) dḥel (feared) ʾellā
(but) šabbeḥ (praised) l-Alāhā (God) b-kulleh (with-all) lebbeh
(his-heart)

*→ But Yukhannan was not afraid; rather he praised God with all his
heart.*

#### Section 9: Grammatical Summary

**THE PAAEL STEM --- FORMATION RULES**

  ------------------ ------------------------- ---------------------------
     **Formation            **Pattern**            **Example (b-r-k)**
      feature**                                

    Root consonant     R1 + R2 DOUBLED + R3              b-rr-k
       pattern                                 

   Perfect 3 m.sg.          CaR₁R₂R₂eR₃                  barrek

   Participle m.sg.       m + CaR₁R₂R₂eR₃                mbarrek

      Participle           -ā, -īn, -ān        mbarrkā, mbarrkīn, mbarrkān
       suffixes                                

     Core meaning     Intensive or Factitive       \'bless solemnly\';
                                                \'sanctify = make holy\'

  ------------------ ------------------------- ---------------------------

**Meaning Pattern**

> **PEAL (basic) + R2 DOUBLED = PAAEL (intensive/factitive)** *\'to X\'
> → \'to X thoroughly\' or \'to make X happen / cause X state\'* q-d-š:
> be holy → qaddaš: sanctify \| b-r-k: bless → barrek: bless solemnly \|
> y-l-p: learn → yallep: teach

**Semitic Language Family Comparison**

  ----------------- ----------------- --------------------- -----------------
     **Syriac**         **Peal**            **Paael**           **Aphel**

       Meaning            Basic        Intensive/Factitive      Causative

  Hebrew (cognate)         Qal                Piel               Hiphil

> **FINAL REMINDER: All three stems --- Peal, Paael, and Aphel --- share
> the same suffix set for the perfect and the same m- prefix for the
> active participle. The stems are distinguished by: (1) Peal: basic
> vowels only; (2) Paael: R2 gemination; (3) Aphel: ʾa- prefix.**
