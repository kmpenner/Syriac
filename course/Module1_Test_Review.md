# Module 1 Test (Lessons 0–4): Review Guide

What to study:

- The 22 letters of the Serto script: their names, sounds and joining behaviour; the vowel signs.
- The three states of the noun (emphatic, absolute, construct) and the plural ending with syāmē.
- Independent pronouns and enclitic pronouns; verbless clauses (ʾenā ṭābā-nā).
- The d- particle (relative and genitive) and the l- preposition (to/for, object marker).
- The Peal active participle as a present tense (kāteb-nā "I am writing").
- The Peal perfect of strong verbs, all persons (ktab, ketbat, ktabt…).
- Pronominal suffixes on nouns (malk-ī, malk-eh, malk-āh…) and on prepositions (lī, leh, lāh, lan).
- Vocabulary of Lessons 0–4 and the story of Parts 1–4.

Format: 18 multiple-choice questions (1 point each) and one translation of unseen Syriac (10 points). Everything in the unseen Syriac uses vocabulary and grammar from the lessons listed. The paradigm tables in Appendix A are the best single review sheet.
