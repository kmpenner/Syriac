# Module 2 Test (Lessons 5–8): Review Guide

What to study:

- The Aphel stem: ʾa- prefix, causative meaning (ʾaqreb "he offered", ʾašdar "he sent"); assimilated Nun (ʾappeq).
- The Paael stem: doubled middle radical; intensive, factitive and denominative meanings (qaddeš, qabbel, ṣallī).
- Participles of the derived stems with m- (mqaddeš, maqreb).
- Weak roots: III-Yodh (ḥzā, ḥzāt, ḥzayt), I-Alaph (ʾemar, nēmar), I-Yodh (īdaʿ, neddaʿ), I-Nun (npaq, neppūq), ʾetā.
- The imperfect: prefixes ʾ-, t-, n- and endings -īn, -ūn, -ān; uses for future, purpose (d-) and wishes.
- Vocabulary of Lessons 5–8 and the story of Parts 5–8.

Format: 16 multiple-choice questions (1 point each) and one translation of unseen Syriac (10 points). Everything in the unseen Syriac uses vocabulary and grammar from the lessons listed. The paradigm tables in Appendix A are the best single review sheet.
